	# EXP-3
EXPT NO: 3				STAIR CASE WIRING                     

 
AIM
 To control the status of the given lamp by using two–way switches. 
APPARATUS REQUIRED:

### APPARATUS REQUIRED

| S. No. | Name of the Apparatus | Range / Type | Quantity |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incandescent Lamp | 60 W | 1 No. |
| 2 | Lamp Holder | Pendant Type | 1 No. |
| 3 | SPDT Switch | 230 V, 5 A | 2 Nos. |
| 4 | Wires | 1/18" | As per requirement |
| 5 | P.V.C. Pipe | 1/4" | As per requirement |
| 6 | Wooden Board | - | 1 No. |
| 7 | Round Block | - | 1 No. |

## Theory

- A two-way switch is installed near the first step of the stairs. The other two-way switch is installed at the upper part where the stairs end.
- The light point is provided between the first and last stair at an adequate location and height. If the light is switched on by the lower switch, it can be switched off by the switch at the top or vice versa.
- The circuit can be used in places like bedrooms where a person may not have to travel to switch off the light at the place from where the light is switched on.
- Two two-way switches are used for this purpose. The supply is given to the switch at the short-circuited terminal.
- The connection to the light point is taken from the similar short-circuited terminal of the second switch. The two independent terminals of each circuit are connected through cables.

## PROCEDURE

- Place the accessories on the wiring board as per the circuit diagram.
- Place the PVC pipe and insert two wires into the PVC pipe.
- Take one wire, connect one end to the phase side and the other end to the middle point of SPDT switch 1.
- Upper point of SPDT switch 1 is connected to the lower point of SPDT switch 2.
- Lower point of SPDT switch 1 is connected to the upper point of SPDT switch 2.
- Another wire taken through a PVC pipe and the middle point of SPDT switch 2 is connected to one end of the lamp holder.
- Another end of the lamp holder is connected to the neutral line.
- Screw the accessories on the board and switch on the supply.
- Circuit is tested for all possible combinations of switch positions.
